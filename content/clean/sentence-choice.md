# Sentence choice

- Type: sentence-choice
- Source: the sentence patterns, speech bubbles and example sentences of Units 1-16.
- Game: Sentence Quiz. The learner reads a sentence with one blank and picks A, B, C or D.

## Rules

- `___` marks the blank. Each sentence has one blank.
- Word is the word id that opens the question. The question comes only when the learner has seen this word.
- Only the answer fits the sentence. The wrong options make a wrong sentence (wrong word type, a missing "a", a plural after "a", and so on).
- The game shows a small picture next to each option that is a word with a picture.
- Each option is different from the answer and from the other options.
