# Unit 10: At the zoo

- Type: unit
- Book pages: 43-45
- PDF pages: 44-46
- Phonics: Mm
- Vocabulary: mango, mother, mouse, monkey
- Names: none printed
- Sentence patterns: That's a monkey. / That's a ____. / That's my mother.

## Lesson 1

### Activity 1: Listen and repeat. (Track 55)

- Words: mango, mother, mouse, monkey
- Highlighted letter: m
- Picture: a zoo with trees; monkeys play, and one holds mangoes; a mother and a girl look over a stone wall; a white mouse runs on the wall; a monkey climbs a ladder.

### Activity 2: Point and say.

- Uses the Activity 1 picture. Point to mango, mother, mouse, monkey and say the words.

## Lesson 2

### Activity 3: Listen and chant. (Track 56)

- Target words: monkey, mouse, mother, mango
- Repeated patterns: "M, m, m." / "M is for ____." / "____ and ____." (Monkey and mouse. / Mother and mango.)
- Highlighted letter: m
- Summary: two verses; each says "M is for ..." for two words, then names both.
- Picture: a mother and a girl look at a monkey on a ladder; a mango hangs in a tree.

### Activity 4: Listen and tick. (Track 57)

1. a) [picture: monkey] b) [picture: mouse] — answer: from audio, not shown
2. a) [picture: red apple] b) [picture: yellow mango] — answer: from audio, not shown

### Activity 5: Look, trace and write.

- Trace: M M M M (capital letter, 4 times)
- Trace: m m m m (small letter, 4 times)
- Missing letters to write:
  - __ango [picture: green mango] — answer (from pictures): m
  - __other [picture: a woman with a pink headscarf] — answer (from pictures): m
  - __onkey [picture: monkey] — answer (from pictures): m
  - __ouse [picture: mouse] — answer (from pictures): m

## Lesson 3

### Activity 6: Listen and repeat. (Track 58)

- Picture: a mother and a girl; the girl points at a monkey that hangs from a tree. Speech: "That's a monkey."

### Activity 7: Let's talk.

- Speech frame: "That's a ____."
- a) [picture: a finger points at a monkey]
- b) [picture: a finger points at a mouse]
- c) [picture: a finger points at a mango]
- d) [picture: a finger points at a blue-green toy horse] (horse is a Unit 8 word)

### Activity 8: Let's sing! (Track 59)

- Target words: monkey, mango, mother
- Repeated patterns: "____, ____." / "That's a ____." / "That's my mother."
- Highlighted letter: m
- Summary: three short verses; each says a word twice, then "That's a ..." or "That's my mother."
- Picture: a mother and a girl at a monkey cage; the girl points at a monkey that hangs from a branch; a mango in the tree.
