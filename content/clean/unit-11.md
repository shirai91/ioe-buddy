# Unit 11: At the bus stop

- Type: unit
- Book pages: 48-50
- PDF pages: 49-51
- Phonics: Uu
- Vocabulary: sun, truck, bus, running
- Names: none
- Sentence patterns: He's running. / She's running.

## Lesson 1

### Activity 1: Listen and repeat. (Track 60)

- Words: sun, truck, bus, running
- Highlighted letter: u
- Picture: a street with a bus stop sign; a yellow school bus stops and two girls with bags get on; a boy with a red bag runs to the bus; a pink truck and a blue car drive on the road; the sun is in the sky; a man walks a dog near the houses; two ducks swim in a pond.

### Activity 2: Point and say.

- Uses the Activity 1 picture: point to the sun, the truck, the bus and the running boy and say the words.

## Lesson 2

### Activity 3: Listen and chant. (Track 61)

- Target words: truck, bus, running, sun
- Repeated patterns: "Look at the ____. The ____ is moving." / "The boy is running."
- Summary: the truck and the bus are moving, and the boy is running in the sun.
- Picture: a boy with a red bag runs by the road; a yellow bus; the sun shines through the trees.

### Activity 4: Listen and tick. (Track 62)

1. a) [picture: green and yellow bus] b) [picture: red truck with a white box] — answer: from audio, not shown
2. a) [picture: red-haired boy with glasses jumping] b) [picture: dark-haired boy running] — answer: from audio, not shown

### Activity 5: Look, trace and write.

- Trace: capital U (4 times), small u (4 times)
- Write the missing letter:
  - [picture: yellow bus] b___s — answer (from pictures): u
  - [picture: yellow and blue truck] tr___ck — answer (from pictures): u
  - [picture: sun] s___n — answer (from pictures): u
  - [picture: boy running] r___nning — answer (from pictures): u

## Lesson 3

### Activity 6: Listen and repeat. (Track 63)

1. He's running.
2. She's running.

- Picture: a red-haired boy in a green shirt and a girl with pigtails in a yellow shirt run together.

### Activity 7: Let's talk.

- Speech bubble: "____'s running."
- a) [picture: girl in pink running] — answer (from pictures): She's running.
- b) [picture: dark-haired boy in an orange striped shirt running] — answer (from pictures): He's running.
- c) [picture: red-haired boy in a green shirt running] — answer (from pictures): He's running.
- d) [picture: girl with pigtails in a yellow shirt running] — answer (from pictures): She's running.

### Activity 8: Let's sing! (Track 64)

- Target words: sunny, running, sun
- Repeated patterns: "It's a sunny day." / "The boy is running."
- Summary: it is a sunny day and the boy is running in the sun.
- Picture: a boy with a red bag runs on the road; a yellow bus with a driver, a car and a truck; a bright sun.
