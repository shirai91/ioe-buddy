## Lesson 1

### Activity 1: Listen and repeat. (Track 41)

- Words: hand, head, horse, hair
- Highlighted letter: h
- Picture: a park with trees and a pond; a man in a yellow cap blows a whistle; a boy with glasses does exercises; a boy raises his hand; a girl with a ponytail raises her hand; a yellow toy rocking horse.

## Lesson 2

### Activity 3: Listen and chant. (Track 42)

- Target words: head, hair, hat, hands
- Repeated patterns: "H, h, h." / "____ and ____." (Head and hair. / Hat and hands.) / "Hoa's ____."
- Highlighted letter: h
- Summary: two short verses that name Hoa's head, hair, hat and hands.
- Picture: a girl with a braid and a yellow hat covers her eyes behind a tree; two boys look out from a bush.

## Lesson 3

### Activity 6: Listen and repeat. (Track 44)

- Picture: a girl with long hair touches her hair. Speech: "Touch your hair."

### Activity 7: Let's talk.

- Speech frame: "Touch your ____."
- a) [picture: girl with two braids touches her hair]
- b) [picture: two hands touch]
- c) [picture: boy touches his head]
- d) [picture: a hand touches a toy rocking horse]

### Activity 8: Let's sing! (Track 45)

- Target words: head, hair, hand, horse
- Repeated patterns: "Your ____ and your ____." / "Touch your ____."
- Highlighted letter: h
- Summary: two verses; each names two things twice, then says "Touch your ..." for each one.
- Picture: a boy touches his head; a girl with two braids touches her hair; a boy touches a blue-green toy rocking horse.
