## Lesson 2

- Repeated patterns: "G, g, ____." / "A ____ and a ____." (A goat and a gate. / A girl and a garden.)

- Summary: a chant that says the letter G, g with each g word, then joins the words in pairs.
- Picture: a girl with a bucket stands at a white gate; a goat eats among orange flowers; a wheelbarrow on the path.

## Lesson 3

### Activity 6: Listen and repeat. (Track 39)

- Picture: a garden with flower beds, a path and a wooden fence. Speech: "There's a garden."

### Activity 7: Let's talk.

- Speech frame: "There's a ____."
- a) [picture: garden with flowers and a wheelbarrow]
- b) [picture: open wooden gate between stone walls]
- c) [picture: girl with a yellow flower in her hair, waving]
- d) [picture: goat]

### Activity 8: Let's sing! (Track 40)

- Target words: girl, goat, garden
- Repeated patterns: "There's a ____ / In the garden." / "A ____ in the garden."
- Highlighted letter: g
- Summary: a song about a girl and a goat in the garden.
- Picture: a girl with a pink hair bow sits under a tree and sings; a goat jumps near a white fence.
