## Lesson 3

### Activity 6: Listen and repeat. (Track 53)

- Picture: two girls sit at a table with two clocks on it. Speech: "How many clocks?" / "Two."

- Speech frame: "How many ____?" / "____."
- a) [picture: two clocks]
- b) [picture: three locks]
- c) [picture: four pots]
- d) [picture: five mops]

### Activity 8: Let's sing! (Track 54)

- Target words: clocks, locks, pots, mops; numbers one to five
- Repeated patterns: "[number], [number]. [number], [number]." / "There are [number] ____."
- Highlighted letter: o
- Summary: the song counts up from one to five: two clocks, three locks, four pots, five mops.
- Picture: in a shop, a woman takes a bottle from a shelf; a boy puts a mop in the shopping cart.
