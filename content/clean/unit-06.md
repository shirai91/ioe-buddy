## Lesson 1

### Activity 1: Listen and repeat. (Track 31)

- Words: red, pencil, pen, bell
- Highlighted letter: e
- Picture: a classroom; a red note on a notice board (red); a boy with orange hair and glasses holds a red pencil (pencil); a red pen on the desk in front of a girl with a brown ponytail (pen); a small yellow bell on the desk (bell); a globe, a clock, bookshelves and a green school bag.

## Lesson 2

### Activity 3: Listen and chant. (Track 32)

- Target words: red, pen, pencil
- Repeated patterns: "E, e, ____." / "E, e, a red ____." (a red pen / a red pencil)
- Highlighted letter: e
- Summary: a chant that says the letter E, e with each e word, then joins them: a red pen, a red pencil.
- Picture: a boy with curly blond hair holds a red pen and talks to a boy with orange hair and glasses who holds a red pencil; a basket of pink flowers.

1. a) [picture: red bell] b) [picture: red pen] — answer: from audio, not shown

## Lesson 3

### Activity 6: Listen and repeat. (Track 34)

- Picture: a boy with curly blond hair at a desk points to a red pen. Speech: "It's a red pen."

### Activity 7: Let's talk.

- Speech frame: "It's a red ____."
- a) [picture: red pencil]
- b) [picture: red pen]
- c) [picture: small red table with two drawers]
- d) [picture: red bell]

### Activity 8: Let's sing! (Track 35)

- Names: Jen, Ben
- Target words: pen, pencil, red
- Repeated patterns: "I have a pen." / "He has a pencil." / "It's red. It's a red ____."
- Highlighted letter: e
- Summary: Jen says she has a red pen, and Ben has a red pencil.
- Picture: a girl with blond hair and a blue hairband holds a red pen and a boy with orange hair and glasses holds a red pencil, in front of a green board; books and rulers on the desk.
