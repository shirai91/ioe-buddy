# Unit 12: At the lake

- Type: unit
- Book pages: 51-53
- PDF pages: 52-54
- Phonics: Ll
- Vocabulary: lake, leaf, lemons
- Names: Lucy
- Sentence patterns: Look at Lucy. / Look at the lemons.

## Lesson 1

### Activity 1: Listen and repeat. (Track 65)

- Words: lake, Lucy, leaf, lemons
- Highlighted letter: l (L in "Lucy")
- Picture: a family has a picnic on a blue checked cloth by a lake; Lucy (a girl with a yellow ribbon) drinks juice; her mother eats a sandwich; her father reads a newspaper labelled "NEWS"; a small boy in blue overalls looks up at a falling green leaf; a basket of lemons; boats on the lake.

### Activity 2: Point and say.

- Uses the Activity 1 picture: point to the lake, Lucy, the leaf and the lemons and say the words.

## Lesson 2

### Activity 3: Listen and chant. (Track 66)

- Target words: Lucy, lake, lemons
- Repeated patterns: "L, l, ____." / "Look at ____." / "Look at the ____."
- Summary: say the letter L with each word, then look at Lucy, the lake and the lemons.
- Picture: the family at the picnic by the lake; Lucy holds up a green leaf and the small boy looks at it; lemons fall out of the basket.

## Lesson 3

### Activity 6: Listen and repeat. (Track 68)

1. Look at Lucy.
2. Look at the lemons.

- Picture: Lucy holds two lemons in her hand.

### Activity 7: Let's talk.

- Speech bubbles: "Look at ____." / "Look at the ____."
- "Look at ____." pictures:
  - a) [picture: Lucy, girl with a yellow ribbon] — answer (from pictures): Look at Lucy.
  - b) [picture: boy with curly red hair]
- "Look at the ____." pictures:
  - a) [picture: basket of lemons] — answer (from pictures): Look at the lemons.
  - b) [picture: lake] — answer (from pictures): Look at the lake.

### Activity 8: Let's sing! (Track 69)

- Target words: Lucy, lake, leaf, falling
- Repeated patterns: "Look at Lucy." / "Look at the leaf."
- Summary: Lucy is running round the lake and a leaf is falling to the ground.
- Picture: Lucy and a small boy run by the lake; a leaf is on the ground; the mother and father sit on a picnic cloth with a basket.
