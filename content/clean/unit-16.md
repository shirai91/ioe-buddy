# Unit 16: At home

- Type: unit
- Book pages: 68-70
- PDF pages: 69-71
- Phonics: Ww
- Vocabulary: window, washing, water, six, seven, eight, nine, ten
- Names: Wendy
- Sentence patterns: How many windows can you see? / I can see six.

## Lesson 1

### Activity 1: Listen and repeat. (Track 89)

- Words: window, washing, Wendy, water
- Highlighted letter: w (W in Wendy)
- Picture: outside a brick house with pink roses over the roof; a girl with red hair (Wendy) stands on a wooden stool and washes a blue window with a yellow sponge; the window has blue shutters; a green bucket of water is next to the stool; a man on a stepladder works on the roses; a white cat sleeps by the green door.

### Activity 2: Point and say.

- Use the Activity 1 picture: point to the window, the washing, Wendy and the water and say the words.

## Lesson 2

### Activity 3: Listen and chant. (Track 90)

- Target words: window, windows; numbers six, seven, eight, nine, ten
- Repeated patterns: "W, w, window." / "How many windows?"
- Summary: a counting chant about the windows of a tall building; the answers count from six up to ten.
- Picture: a tall orange brick building with ten windows (two columns of five).

### Activity 4: Listen and tick. (Track 91)

1. a) [picture: brick building with six windows] six windows b) [picture: ten lemons] ten lemons — answer: from audio, not shown
2. a) [picture: seven spinning tops] seven tops b) [picture: eight pens] eight pens — answer: from audio, not shown

### Activity 5: Look, trace and write.

- Trace: W W W W (capital), w w w w (small)
- Write the missing letter:
  1. [picture: girl with red hair, Wendy] ___endy — missing letter: W
  2. [picture: pink bucket of water] ___ater — missing letter: w
  3. [picture: hand washes a window with a yellow sponge] ___ashing — missing letter: w
  4. [picture: blue window] ___indow — missing letter: w

## Lesson 3

### Activity 6: Listen and repeat. (Track 92)

- Picture: a boy in a yellow T-shirt and a girl in a pink T-shirt look at a brick wall with six windows.
- Speech (boy): "How many windows can you see?"
- Speech (girl): "I can see six."

### Activity 7: Let's talk.

- Frame: "How many ____ can you see?" / "I can see ____."
- a) [picture: brick wall with six windows] — count (from picture): six
- b) [picture: red pens] — count (from picture): seven
- c) [picture: stack of coloured books] — count (from picture): eight
- d) [picture: red pencils] — count (from picture): nine
- e) [picture: yellow lemons] — count (from picture): ten

### Activity 8: Let's sing! (Track 93)

- Target words: Wendy, window; numbers one to ten; bedroom door, hat, bed, cat
- Repeated pattern: "She can see a ___ ..."
- Summary: a counting song; Wendy is at her bedroom door and she can see a hat on her bed and a cat at the window.
- Picture: Wendy opens a door into a bedroom; a hat is on the bed and a white cat is at the window.

## Notes

- Activity 2 has no track number on the page.
- Activity 7: the page prints no numbers. The counts are from the pictures (six, seven, eight, nine, ten). The books in c) overlap, so eight is the best count.
- Activity 1 uses "washing"; the glossary lists "wash" (U 16).
