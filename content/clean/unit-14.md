# Unit 14: In the toy shop

- Type: unit
- Book pages: 60-62
- PDF pages: 61-63
- Phonics: Tt
- Vocabulary: teddy bear, top, tiger, turtle
- Names: Tony
- Sentence patterns: I can see a tiger.

## Lesson 1

### Activity 1: Listen and repeat. (Track 79)

- Words: teddy bear, top, tiger, turtle
- Highlighted letter: t
- Picture: a toy shop with bunting; a red-haired boy holds a spinning top at the window; a teddy bear on a shelf; a toy tiger on a pink rug; a toy turtle; other toys (elephant, lion, giraffe, robot, rocket, clown, car).

### Activity 2: Point and say.

- Uses the Activity 1 picture: point to teddy bear, top, tiger, turtle and say the words.

## Lesson 2

### Activity 3: Listen and chant. (Track 80)

- Target words: top, turtle, tiger, teddy bear, Tony
- Repeated patterns: "T, t, ___." / "Tony has a ___."
- Summary: Tony has a top, a turtle, a tiger and a teddy bear.
- Picture: a red-haired boy sits with a toy turtle, a teddy bear, a toy tiger and a top.

### Activity 4: Listen and tick. (Track 81)

1. a) [picture: teddy bear] b) [picture: spinning top] — answer: from audio, not shown
2. a) [picture: toy tiger] b) [picture: toy turtle] — answer: from audio, not shown

### Activity 5: Look, trace and write.

- Trace: capital T (4 times), small t (4 times)
- Write the missing letter:
  - [picture: spinning top] ___op (missing: t)
  - [picture: teddy bear] ___eddy bear (missing: t)
  - [picture: toy turtle] ___urtle (missing: t)
  - [picture: toy tiger] ___iger (missing: t)

## Lesson 3

### Activity 6: Listen and repeat. (Track 82)

- Speech: "I can see a tiger."
- Picture: a girl with a yellow hair band waves at a toy shelf next to a Christmas tree; a toy tiger is on top of the shelf.

### Activity 7: Let's talk.

- Speech bubble: "I can see a ____."
- a) [picture: toy tiger] b) [picture: spinning top] c) [picture: blue teddy bear in a Santa hat] d) [picture: toy turtle]

### Activity 8: Let's sing! (Track 83)

- Target words: Tony, toy shop, teddy bear, tiger
- Repeated patterns: "I can see ___." / "He's in the toy shop." / "It's in the toy shop."
- Summary: Tony is in the toy shop holding a teddy bear, and a toy tiger is on the shelf.
- Picture: a red-haired boy hugs a big teddy bear next to a Christmas tree and toy shelves; a toy tiger is on a shelf.
