## Lesson 2

### Activity 3: Listen and chant. (Track 27)

- Target words: fish, chips, milk, chicken
- Repeated patterns: "I, i, ____." / "____ and ____." (Fish and chips. / Milk and chicken.)
- Highlighted letter: i
- Summary: a chant that says the letter I, i with each i word, then joins the words in pairs.
- Picture: a girl and a boy eat at a table outside the shop; chips, fish and chicken on the table; a dog sits next to the girl; a football on the ground; a sign "FISH & CHIPS" on the shop.

## Lesson 3

- Repeated patterns: "Fish and chips." / "Milk and chicken." / "I like ____ and ____."
