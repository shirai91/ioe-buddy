# Unit 2: In the dining room

- Type: unit
- Book pages: 9-11
- PDF pages: 10-12
- Phonics: Cc
- Vocabulary: cake, car, cat, cup
- Names: none printed
- Sentence patterns: I have a car.

## Lesson 1

### Activity 1: Listen and repeat. (Track 7)

- Words: cake, car, cup, cat
- Highlighted letter: c
- Picture: a family in a dining room; a mother eats cake; a boy plays with a red toy car on the table; a father drinks from a cup; a blue cat sits under a chair; a green cupboard with glasses and bottles.

### Activity 2: Point and say.

- Uses the Activity 1 picture. Point to cake, car, cup, cat and say the words.

## Lesson 2

### Activity 3: Listen and chant. (Track 8)

- Target words: cup, cake, cat, car
- Repeated pattern: "C, c, a cup. / C, c, a cake. / A cup and a cake." (same pattern for cat and car)
- Summary: a chant that says the letter C, c and then pairs of c words.
- Picture: a boy eats a cake at a table with fruit and a cup; a cat and a toy car behind a plant.

### Activity 4: Listen and tick. (Track 9)

1. a) [picture: blue cat] b) [picture: green toy car] — answer: from audio, not shown
2. a) [picture: white cup] b) [picture: cupcake with a cherry] — answer: from audio, not shown

### Activity 5: Look and trace.

- Trace: C C C C (capital letter, 4 times)
- Trace: c c c c (small letter, 4 times)
- Missing letters to write: none

## Lesson 3

### Activity 6: Listen and repeat. (Track 10)

- Picture: a boy with curly orange hair holds up a green toy car. Speech: "I have a car."

### Activity 7: Let's talk.

- Speech frame: "I have a ____."
- a) [picture: red toy car]
- b) [picture: cake with strawberries]
- c) [picture: pink cup]
- d) [picture: grey kitten]

### Activity 8: Let's sing! (Track 11)

- Target words: cup, car, cake, cat
- Repeated patterns: "I have a cup." / "I have a cup and I have a car."
- Highlighted letter: c
- Summary: a child sings about the things he has: a cup, a car, a cake, a cat.
- Picture: a boy sits on a green sofa with a blue cat; shelves with toys behind him.
