# Unit 13: In the school canteen

- Type: unit
- Book pages: 57-59
- PDF pages: 58-60
- Phonics: Nn
- Vocabulary: noodles, nuts, bananas
- Names: Nick, Nam
- Sentence patterns: He's having nuts. / She's having noodles.

## Lesson 1

### Activity 1: Listen and repeat. (Track 74)

- Words: Nick, noodles, bananas, nuts
- Highlighted letter: n (N in "Nick")
- Picture: a school canteen with the sign "SCHOOL CANTEEN"; two cooks behind the counter; a blond boy (Nick) eats noodles from a bowl with chopsticks; a banana bunch on his pink tray; a brown-haired boy eats nuts from a bag; other children eat at tables.

### Activity 2: Point and say.

- Uses the Activity 1 picture: point to Nick, noodles, bananas, nuts and say the words.

## Lesson 2

### Activity 3: Listen and chant. (Track 75)

- Target words: Nam, nuts, Nick, noodles
- Repeated patterns: "Nam, Nam, Nam." / "Nam's having nuts." / "Nick's having noodles."
- Summary: Nam is having nuts and Nick is having noodles.
- Picture: three boys at a table in the school canteen; one boy eats nuts from a bag; a blond boy has a bowl of noodles; a cook carries a tray.

1. a) [picture: bowl of noodles] b) [picture: bag of nuts] — answer: from audio, not shown

## Lesson 3

### Activity 6: Listen and repeat. (Track 77)

1. He's having nuts.
2. She's having noodles.

- Picture: a red-haired boy eats nuts at a table; a girl with pigtails eats noodles from a bowl.

### Activity 7: Let's talk.

- Speech bubbles: "He's having ____." / "She's having ____."
- He pictures: a) [picture: red-haired boy eating nuts] b) [picture: boy eating chips]
- She pictures: a) [picture: girl eating noodles] b) [picture: girl eating bananas]

### Activity 8: Let's sing! (Track 78)

- Target words: Nam, nuts, Nick, noodles
- Repeated patterns: "There's Nam." / "Nam's having nuts." / "He's having nuts today." / "There's Nick." / "Nick's having noodles." / "He's having noodles today."
- Summary: Nam is having nuts and Nick is having noodles today.
- Picture: two boys at a table in a kitchen; one eats nuts from a bag, the other eats noodles from a bowl.
