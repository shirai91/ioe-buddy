# Unit 1: In the school playground

- Type: unit
- Book pages: 6-8
- PDF pages: 7-9
- Phonics: Bb
- Vocabulary: ball, bike, book
- Names: Bill, Ba
- Sentence patterns: Hi, I'm Bill. / Bye, Bill.

## Lesson 1

### Activity 1: Listen and repeat. (Track 2)

- Words: ball, Bill, book, bike
- Highlighted letter: b (B in Bill)
- Picture: school playground; a school building with a Vietnamese flag; two boys play with a basketball (the boy with curly orange hair is labelled Bill); a girl sits under a tree and reads a red book; an orange bike stands among pink flowers; birds in the tree.

### Activity 2: Point and say.

- Uses the Activity 1 picture. Point to ball, Bill, book, bike and say the words.

## Lesson 2

### Activity 3: Listen and chant. (Track 3)

- Target words: ball, book, bike
- Repeated pattern: "B, b, ball. / A ball, a ball. / B, b, a ball." (same pattern for book and bike)
- Summary: a chant that says the letter B, b and then each b word.
- Picture: a boy sits on a bench with a school bag; a bike behind him; a football goal with a ball.

### Activity 4: Listen and tick. (Track 4)

1. a) [picture: orange basketball] b) [picture: orange bike] — example answer shown: b
2. a) [picture: red and white football] b) [picture: book, the Tieng Anh 1 textbook] — answer: from audio, not shown

### Activity 5: Look and trace.

- Trace: B B B B (capital letter, 4 times)
- Trace: b b b b (small letter, 4 times)
- Missing letters to write: none

## Lesson 3

### Activity 6: Listen and repeat. (Track 5)

- Picture 1: a boy with curly orange hair (Bill) waves and points to himself. Speech: "Hi, I'm Bill."
- Picture 2: a boy with black hair and a backpack waves goodbye (back view). Speech: "Bye, Bill."

### Activity 7: Let's talk.

- Speech frames: "Hi, I'm ____." / "Bye, ____."
- Picture 1: a) a boy with curly orange hair in a red shirt points to himself and talks; b) a boy with black hair in a yellow shirt with a backpack listens. Speech: "Hi, I'm ____."
- Picture 2: a) the boy with black hair and a backpack waves (back view); b) the boy with curly orange hair waves. Speech: "Bye, ____."

### Activity 8: Let's sing! (Track 6)

- Names: Ba, Bill
- Repeated patterns: "Hi, I'm Ba." / "Hi, Bill. I'm Ba."
- Highlighted letter: B in Ba and Bill
- Summary: two children, Ba and Bill, say hello and tell their names.
- Picture: children dance on a hill next to a building with the sign "School".
