## Lesson 1

### Activity 1: Listen and repeat. (Track 89)

- Words: window, washing, Wendy, water
- Highlighted letter: w (W in Wendy)
- Picture: outside a brick house with pink roses over the roof; a girl (Wendy) stands on a stool and washes a blue window with a yellow sponge; a green bucket of water; a man on a ladder works on the roses; a white cat sleeps by the green door.

### Activity 2: Point and say.

- Use the Activity 1 picture: point to the window, the washing, Wendy and the water and say the words.

## Lesson 2

- Picture: a tall orange brick building with many windows.

## Lesson 3

### Activity 6: Listen and repeat. (Track 92)

- Picture: a boy and a girl look at a brick wall with six windows.
- Speech (boy): "How many windows can you see?"
- Speech (girl): "I can see six."

### Activity 7: Let's talk.

- Frame: "How many ____ can you see?" / "I can see ____."
- a) [picture: windows, six]
- b) [picture: red pens]
- c) [picture: stack of books]
- d) [picture: red pencils]
- e) [picture: yellow lemons]

### Activity 8: Let's sing! (Track 93)

- Target words: Wendy, window; numbers one to ten; bedroom door, hat, bed, cat
- Repeated pattern: "She can see a ___ ..."
- Summary: a counting song; Wendy is at her bedroom door and she can see a hat on her bed and a cat at the window.
- Picture: Wendy opens a door into a bedroom with a bed, a hat on the bed and a cat at the window.
