## Lesson 2

### Activity 3: Listen and chant. (Track 61)

- Target words: truck, bus, running, sun
- Repeated patterns: "Look at the truck. The truck is moving." / "Look at the bus. The bus is moving." / "Look at the boy. The boy is running." / "The boy is running in the sun."
- Summary: the truck and the bus are moving, and the boy is running in the sun.
- Picture: a boy with a red bag runs on the road; a yellow bus and a pink truck; the sun shines through the trees.

### Activity 8: Let's sing! (Track 64)

- Target words: sunny, running, sun
- Repeated patterns: "It's a sunny day." / "It's sunny today." / "The boy is running." / "The boy is running in the sun."
- Summary: it is a sunny day and the boy is running in the sun.
- Picture: a boy with a red bag runs on the road; a yellow bus with a driver, a car and a truck; a bright sun.
