# Unit 3: At the street market

- Type: unit
- Book pages: 14-16
- PDF pages: 15-17
- Phonics: Aa
- Vocabulary: hat, apple, can, bag
- Names: Ann
- Sentence patterns: This is my bag. / This is my ___.

## Lesson 1

### Activity 1: Listen and repeat. (Track 12)

- Words: hat, apple, can, bag
- Highlighted letter: a
- Picture: street market; a fruit seller in a green hat stands behind a fruit stall (pears, apples, grapes, watermelons); a woman with glasses holds a red apple; a girl holds a can labelled "Apple" and carries a pink bag with a white rabbit on it.

### Activity 2: Point and say.

- Words: hat, apple, can, bag (same picture as Activity 1)

## Lesson 2

### Activity 3: Listen and chant. (Track 13)

- Target words: apple, bag, cat, hat
- Repeated patterns: "A, a, apple." / "There's an apple / In the bag." / "There's a cat / On the hat."
- Summary: a chant on the letter a; an apple is in the bag and a cat is on the hat.
- Picture: a woman with glasses drinks tea at a table with a fruit plate; a girl sits at the table with a can; a cat sleeps on a chair on a yellow hat; a pink bag on the table.

### Activity 4: Listen and tick. (Track 14)

1. a) [picture: orange can labelled "Orange"] b) [picture: green apple] — answer: from audio, not shown
2. a) [picture: yellow shopping bag] b) [picture: green hat] — answer: from audio, not shown

### Activity 5: Look and trace.

- Trace: A A A A (capital A, 4 times)
- Trace: a a a a (lowercase a, 4 times)
- Missing letters to write: none

## Lesson 3

### Activity 6: Listen and repeat. (Track 15)

- Picture: a girl holds a pink bag with a white rabbit on it. Speech: "This is my bag."

### Activity 7: Let's talk.

- Prompt: "This is my ___."
- a) [picture: pink bag with a white rabbit]
- b) [picture: blue hat]
- c) [picture: red apple]
- d) [picture: orange can labelled "Orange"]

### Activity 8: Let's sing! (Track 16)

- Target words: Ann, apple, hat, bag, can
- Repeated patterns: "Hi, I'm Ann." / "This is my apple." / "This is my ___."
- Summary: Ann says hi and shows her apple, hat, bag and can.
- Picture: a girl at a table holds a red apple; on the table are a fruit plate, a pink rabbit bag, a yellow hat and an orange can.

## Notes

- Boundary matches the section map (book pages 14-16, PDF pages 15-17).
- The name "Ann" comes from the song in Activity 8. The girl in Lessons 1-3 is not named elsewhere on these pages.
- The highlighted letter in the song is the "A" / "a" in Ann, apple, hat, bag, can.
