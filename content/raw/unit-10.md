## Lesson 1

- Words: mango, mother, mouse, monkey
- Highlighted letter: m

### Activity 2: Point and say.

## Lesson 2

### Activity 3: Listen and chant. (Track 56)

- Target words: monkey, mouse, mother, mango
- Repeated patterns: "M, m, m." / "M is for monkey." / "M is for mouse." / "Monkey and mouse." / "M is for mother." / "M is for mango." / "Mother and mango."
- Summary: two verses; each says "M is for ..." for two words, then names both.
- Picture: a mother and a girl look at monkeys on a ladder; a mango hangs in a tree.

- Trace: M M M M (capital M, four times)

## Lesson 3

### Activity 6: Listen and repeat. (Track 58)

- Speech: "That's a monkey."
- Picture: a mother and a girl; the girl points at a monkey that hangs from a tree.

### Activity 7: Let's talk.

- Prompt bubble: "That's a ____."
- a) [picture: a finger points at a monkey]
- b) [picture: a finger points at a mouse]
- c) [picture: a finger points at a mango]
- d) [picture: a finger points at a toy horse]

### Activity 8: Let's sing! (Track 59)

- Target words: monkey, mango, mother
- Repeated patterns: "Monkey, monkey." / "That's a monkey." / "Mango, mango." / "That's a mango." / "Mother, mother." / "That's my mother."
- Summary: three short verses; each says a word twice, then "That's a ..." or "That's my mother."
- Picture: a mother and a girl at a monkey cage; a monkey hangs from a branch; a mango in the tree.
