- Words: red, pencil, pen, bell
- Highlighted letter: e

## Lesson 2

### Activity 3: Listen and chant. (Track 32)

- Target words: red, pen, pencil
- Repeated pattern: "E, e, red. / E, e, pen. / E, e, a red pen." (same pattern for pencil: "E, e, a red pencil.")
- Summary: a chant that says the letter E, e and the e words, then joins them: a red pen, a red pencil.
- Picture: a boy with curly blond hair holds a red pen and talks to a boy with orange hair and glasses who holds a red pencil; a basket of pink flowers.

### Activity 4: Listen and tick. (Track 33)

1. a) [picture: red bell] b) [picture: red pen] — answer: from audio, not shown
2. a) [picture: red pen] b) [picture: red pencil] — answer: from audio, not shown

### Activity 5: Look and trace.

- Trace: E E E E (capital letter, 4 times)
- Trace: e e e e (small letter, 4 times)
- Missing letters to write: none

## Lesson 3

### Activity 6: Listen and repeat. (Track 34)

- Picture: a boy with curly blond hair at a desk points to a red pen. Speech: "It's a red pen."

### Activity 7: Let's talk.

- Speech frame: "It's a red ____."
- a) [picture: red pencil]
- b) [picture: red pen]
- c) [picture: small red table or desk]
- d) [picture: red bell]

### Activity 8: Let's sing! (Track 35)

- Names: Jen, Ben
- Target words: pen, pencil, red
- Repeated patterns: "Hello, I'm Jen. I have a pen." / "It's red. It's a red pen." / "This is Ben. He has a pencil." / "It's a red pencil."
- Highlighted letter: e
- Summary: Jen says she has a red pen, and Ben has a red pencil.
- Picture: a girl with blond hair holds a red pen and a boy with orange hair and glasses holds a red pencil, in front of a green board; books and a ruler on the desk.
